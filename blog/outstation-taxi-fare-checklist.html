Outstation Taxi Fare: 9 Questions to Ask Before Booking

Understand trip type, distance, driver bata, tolls, permits and other possible charges before confirming an outstation taxi.

🧾

Travel planning · Get Taxi Kovai

A route price alone may not explain every part of a taxi booking. Before confirming an outstation trip, ask these questions and save the answers.

Questions to ask

  1. Is the trip one-way, round trip or a tour package?
  2. What pickup and destination addresses does the quote cover?
  3. How many kilometres and hours are included?
  4. Is driver bata included or charged separately?
  5. Are tolls and parking included?
  6. Are state permits or interstate charges applicable?
  7. What are the extra-kilometre and extra-hour rates?
  8. Are waiting time and intermediate stops included?
  9. What are the cancellation and rescheduling terms?

Do not assume a route card is a live calculator

Route cards provide reference prices, but the trip type and itinerary may affect the total. Get confirmation from the team before travel, especially if you have several stops or a return journey.
