One-way drop fare policy

The tariff information supplied for this project shows a rate of ₹15/km and fixed driver bata of ₹500 for one-way drops over 130 km, with a stated minimum coverage of 130 km. The same policy text says drops under 100 km are calculated using round-trip mileage at ₹30/km. The wording does not clearly define the 100–130 km band, so this website does not automatically calculate a one-way quote.

Confirm before booking: Share your exact destination and ask the team which tariff rule applies. Tolls, state permits, parking and other applicable charges may be extra.

What to share

  • Pickup and final destination
  • Travel date and preferred departure time
  • Passenger and luggage count
  • Whether any stops are required
  • Confirmation of driver bata, tolls, permits and parking
