Terms & booking information

Please confirm all booking details directly with Get Taxi Kovai before travel.

Enquiries are not confirmed bookings

Submitting the website form opens WhatsApp. It does not create a confirmed reservation, guarantee a vehicle or dispatch a driver. A booking is confirmed only after direct communication with the team.

Fare and availability

Displayed route prices are reference information transcribed from supplied route cards. They may not apply to every trip type or itinerary. Confirm the current total fare, vehicle, date, pickup, destination, included distance and duration before travelling.

Additional charges

Depending on the confirmed trip, tolls, state permits, parking, driver bata, waiting, extra kilometres, extra hours or other charges may apply. Ask for inclusions and exclusions in advance.

Travel conditions

Travel time, route conditions, weather, traffic, road closures and vehicle availability can change. For hill stations and interstate travel, confirm any route-specific arrangements directly with the team.

Changes and cancellation

Ask the team about the applicable cancellation, rescheduling, delay and refund terms when the booking is confirmed. This website does not publish a separate cancellation policy because one was not provided for this project.
