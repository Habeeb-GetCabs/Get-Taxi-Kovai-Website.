Get Taxi Kovai FAQs

Answers to common questions about fare enquiries, hourly rental and booking confirmation.

How do I book a taxi?

Call 90437 43777 or use a WhatsApp enquiry link. Share your pickup, destination, date, trip type and passenger count. The booking is confirmed only after the team responds.

Is the website enquiry form an automatic booking?

No. It opens WhatsApp with the trip details you entered. It does not automatically dispatch a driver or confirm availability.

What is the Sedan hourly rental rate?

The confirmed published rate is ₹350 per hour with 10 km included per hour. Confirm extra-kilometre, extra-hour, waiting and other applicable terms before travel.

What are the hourly prices for SUVs and larger vehicles?

These categories show “Call for best price.” The team confirms pricing based on trip duration, distance, vehicle availability and your requirements.

Are route prices guaranteed?

Route prices are transcribed from supplied route cards and are for reference. Confirm whether the amount applies to your exact trip type and itinerary, plus any extra charges.

Are tolls, parking and permits included?

Do not assume they are included. Ask the team which expenses are included in the confirmed quote.

Can I get an airport taxi?

Yes, you can enquire about pickup and drop-off at Coimbatore International Airport. Share flight timing, meeting point, passengers and luggage.
