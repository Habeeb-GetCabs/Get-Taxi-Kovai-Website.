Privacy policy

How this website handles information you choose to share.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Information you enter

The trip enquiry form asks for trip type, pickup, destination and travel date. When you submit it, the website opens WhatsApp with the details you entered so you can choose whether to send the message. This static website does not transmit the form to a website server.

WhatsApp and phone calls

If you choose to call or message us, the relevant phone or messaging service handles that communication under its own terms and privacy policy. Avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not needed to arrange your journey.

Cookies and analytics

This project does not include analytics IDs, advertising tags or a custom cookie-tracking system by default. If analytics or advertising conversion tracking is added later, this policy should be updated to accurately explain what is collected and how it is used before launch.
